Frequently asked questions

Questions homeowners often ask

Can HVAC equipment improve indoor air quality?

Yes. Filtration, humidity control and ventilation can all play a role, depending on the home and existing system.

Is a whole-home humidifier useful in Michigan?

Many homes feel especially dry during heating season. A whole-home humidifier can help maintain more comfortable indoor humidity.
