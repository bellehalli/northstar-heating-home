Seasonal homeowner tips

Simple habits that support your HVAC system.

1

Check the Filter

Inspect filters regularly and replace them according to the filter and equipment recommendations.

2

Keep Vents Open

Keep supply and return vents clear of furniture, rugs and other airflow obstructions.

3

Clear Outdoor Equipment

Keep leaves, weeds and loose debris away from the outdoor unit without opening or servicing the equipment yourself.

4

Watch for Changes

New noises, odors, short cycling or sudden comfort changes are good reasons to schedule an evaluation.

Maintenance FAQs

How often should HVAC maintenance be scheduled?

Many homeowners schedule heating maintenance before winter and cooling maintenance before summer.

Do I have to join the Comfort Club?

No. One-time tune-ups remain available; the membership simply bundles recurring benefits.

Does maintenance guarantee nothing will break?

No. Maintenance helps identify wear and readiness issues, but mechanical equipment can still fail unexpectedly.
