Frequently asked questions

Questions homeowners often ask

How quickly can heating service be scheduled?

Availability varies with the season. Call or submit a request and Northstar will confirm the next available service window.

What should I do if I smell gas?

Leave the affected area and contact the appropriate gas utility or emergency service. Do not rely on a normal HVAC web form for a possible gas emergency.

When should I consider replacement?

Age, repair frequency, comfort issues and energy use all matter. The replacement page walks through the main decision points.
