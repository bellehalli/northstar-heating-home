Portfolio policies

Privacy & Accessibility

These notes exist so the concept site behaves responsibly as a portfolio project. A real client would receive attorney/client-approved policies appropriate to its tools and data practices.

Privacy

This demo does not send or store form submissions. No production analytics, CRM, advertising pixels, call tracking, cookies or third-party lead systems are connected.

A real launch would document the client’s actual data collection, processors, retention practices and contact methods.

Accessibility

The interface includes semantic headings, keyboard-visible focus, labeled forms, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts and contrast-conscious design.

Production accessibility would include content review and testing against the client’s final copy, media, integrations and browser/device requirements.
