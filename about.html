Our story

Michigan comfort, without the runaround.

Founded in the concept in 2011, Northstar grew around one simple frustration: homeowners should not need to understand HVAC to feel confident hiring an HVAC company.

The brand promise is expressed through clear options, respectful service, straightforward communication and a website built around homeowner intent.
